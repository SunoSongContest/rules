# 🎶 Song requirements

Follow these rules so we can all enjoy the music. **Songs or participants that violate the rules may disqualified at the committee’s discretion**.

* ### 🎵 1. One Song per Person  
  Each participant can submit **only one song** for SSC7.

* ### 🔒 2. No Changes Allowed  
  Do **not** modify your song (lyrics, title, image, etc.) during the contest, unless explicitly required by the staff.

* ### 🌍 3. Language  
  - Songs must be **instrumental** or in a **language recognized by Google Translate**.  
  - **Mixed languages** are allowed.

* ### 📝 4. Lyrics  
  - **Full lyrics** must be visible during the entire contest.
  - Lyrics must comply with **Suno’s Terms of Service**:  
  - They must be **original** or from the **public domain** (e.g., a poem by an author who died over 70 years ago).

* ### ⏱️ 5. Duration  
  - Songs must be between **2:00 and 5:00 minutes** in length.  
  - **Silence at the end** doesn’t count toward duration, as long as the music itself ends by 5:00.

* ### 🏷️ 6. Title and Tag Format  
  - Titles must **not** include emojis, decorative fonts, or special characters.  
  - Every title must end in the following format:  
    `Song Name [SSC7, Country]`  
    Use the **English name** of the country. Only more known acronyms like `USA`, `UK`, `UAE` are accepted.
    **Tip:** Keep your title under **55 characters** to avoid truncation in voting polls.

* ### 🔞 7. PG-13 Content & Copyright  
  **All visible content** (lyrics, title, image, style prompt, author name) must follow PG-13 standards:  
  - ❌ No expletives or foul language  
  - ❌ No drug use, racism, discrimination, or political references  
  - ❌ No direct references to copyrighted or trademarked material  
  - ✅ AI hallucinations are still subject to these rules

* ### ✅ 8. Compliance  
  - All songs will be reviewed for compliance.  
  - If your submission doesn’t meet the rules, we’ll request corrections—**with limited time to fix them**.  
  - Songs may still be disqualified later if rule-breaking content is discovered.  
  - **Intentional violations** (e.g., hiding lyrics, offensive language) may result in bans from future SSCs.

* ### 🔚 9. Song Ending  
  Songs must have a **proper ending**.  
  No abrupt terminations (e.g., cutting off mid-note).

* ### ♻️ 10. No Previous SSC Songs  
  You may submit **previously published songs**, as long as they haven’t competed in **any prior SSC season**, including challenge tracks.

* ### 🚫 11. No Other Creator Challenges  
  To avoid overlap with **official Suno challenges**, SSC7 will **not accept** songs made for:
  - Timbaland Remix Challenge  
  - Halloween specials  
  - Or any other branded creator contests

Stick to the rules, and let’s make SSC7 the best one yet! 🎉
