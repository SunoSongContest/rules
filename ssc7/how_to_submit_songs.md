# 📩 How to submit songs

Fill in the official form. We’ll contact you if corrections are needed or to confirm your song has been accepted.  
Then, wait for the **weekly reveals** to find out when your song will be played!  

### 🆕 New in SSC7

For SSC7, as we did in SSC6, we’ll be using **Google Forms** to submit song info and vote!

* 📎 **The link to the form will be shared when the submission period opens.**
* 🛑 Only songs submitted through the **official form** will be accepted.
* 💬 You’re encouraged to share your song in the Discord chat, but that alone is **not enough**—the form is mandatory.

### 📋 What You'll Need to Submit

When filling out the form, you’ll be asked to provide:

* **Your Discord username**  
  So we know how to reach you for updates and approval.
* **Your Suno username & the link to your song**  
  So we know exactly which song you’re submitting.
* **Background info about your song**  
  This helps us promote your track and celebrate your success if you reach the finals!

### 🔍 Review Process

Once submitted, we will:

* **Check your song** to make sure it follows all the rules.  
* If we find issues (e.g. incorrect title, duration outside limits), we’ll contact you.
* You will have **24 hours** to correct and resubmit.

### ✅ Acceptance & Confirmation

After your song is approved, you’ll receive a confirmation message letting you know you’ve made it into **SSC7**! 🎉

This message will also contain your **voting password**, which is required to vote during the contest.  
➡️ **Keep it secret. Keep it safe.**

### 📣 Important Reminders

* After submitting the form, **send a direct message** to one of the following staff members:  
  `@JoshuaSodil`, `@teemuth`, or `@jonathankennedy`  
  This ensures we can contact you about your song’s status.
* The **weekly playlist reveals** will remain a surprise!  
  📺 Stay tuned every **live-stream** to discover which songs are featured each week.

Let’s make this the most exciting SSC yet—good luck and have fun!
