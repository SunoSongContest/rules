# 📥 How to vote

Fill in the weekly form, enter your voting password, and vote **Eurovision-style**!  

For SSC7, as we did in SSC6, all votes will be cast using **Google Forms**.  
Every **Thursday at 12 p.m. UTC**, a new voting form will be shared, listing all the songs from that week.

🗓️ **The voting will be open for 7 days and will close next Thursday at 12 p.m. UTC.**  
🛑 Only people who submitted a song for SSC7 can vote.

### 🔑 Voting Password

Each accepted participant will receive a **voting password**.

- You must enter this password in the voting form to cast your vote.
- Please **keep your password safe and confidential**.
- If you lose or forget your password, contact a committee member directly.

### 🧮 Points-Based Voting

If you’ve watched Eurovision, you’ll feel right at home!  
“**Douze Points!!**”

You’ll award points to your favorite songs as follows:

- **12 points** – your top favorite
- **10 points** – your second favorite
- **8 to 1 points** – one song for each point level

✅ You cannot vote for your own song.

### 🔁 Voting Streak Bonus 
Starting with SSC7, voting consistently each week will reward you with **bonus points**!

Each consecutive week you vote, you’ll earn an increasing number of points:

- **Week 1** = +1 point  
- **Week 2** = +2 points  
- **Week 3** = +3 points  
- **Week 4** = +4 points  
- **Week 5** = +5 points  

That’s a total of **15 bonus points** available if you vote every week!  

> 🧠 **Example**: If you vote only in Week 1 and Week 3, you’ll get just **1 + 1 = 2 bonus points**.

These streak bonuses will be added to your overall score and may help you qualify for the finals.  
So stay active and keep voting each week!
